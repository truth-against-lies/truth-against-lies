## A1. The Protocols of the Elders of Zion -- "Jewish World Domination Plan"

**What is claimed:**
A secret cabal of Jewish leaders met at the First Zionist Congress in Basel, Switzerland (1897) and produced a document outlining a 24-point plan to take over the world. The plan allegedly includes: controlling the press, manipulating financial markets, undermining Christian civilization, fomenting wars, corrupting youth, and establishing a single world government under Jewish rule.

**Origin:**
- First published in Russia in 1903 by Sergei Nilus
- Actually fabricated by agents of the Russian Tsarist secret police (Okhrana)
- Plagiarized from TWO non-Jewish sources:
  1. Maurice Joly's 1864 satirical dialogue about Napoleon III ("Dialogue aux enfers entre Machiavel et Montesquieu")
  2. Hermann Goedsche's 1868 novel "Biarritz"
- The fabrication was designed to blame Jews for Russia's social problems and justify anti-Jewish policies

**Debunking:**
- **1921**: Philip Graves of The Times (London) proved it was a forgery by showing paragraph-by-paragraph parallels with Joly's 1864 satire
- **1934-35**: A Swiss court in Bern declared the Protocols "forgeries," "plagiarisms," and "ridiculous nonsense"
- Russian historian Vladimir Burtsev traced the forgery directly to the Okhrana
- Adolf Hitler and the Nazi leadership KNEW it was fake but used it anyway. Hitler wrote in Mein Kampf that its truth was "guaranteed by the fact" that it revealed Jewish plans -- circular logic
- Every scholarly investigation in 100+ years has confirmed it is a forgery

**Grain of truth being distorted:** NONE. It is a complete fabrication from start to finish. The only "truth" is that Zionist congresses existed -- but their proceedings were public and had nothing to do with world domination.

**How widespread:**
- The most widely distributed antisemitic publication in modern history
- Translated into every major language
- Required reading in Nazi Germany
- Still sold openly in many Arab and Muslim-majority countries
- Cited in Hamas's 1988 Charter (Article 32)
- Continues to circulate online with millions of views annually

## A2. The Rothschild Banking Conspiracy

**What is claimed:**
The Rothschild family secretly controls the world's central banks, orchestrates wars for profit, manipulates global financial markets, owns most of the world's wealth, and pulls the strings of governments worldwide. Specific sub-claims include:
- Nathan Rothschild profited from advance knowledge of the Battle of Waterloo (1815)
- The family "owns" the Federal Reserve, Bank of England, and other central banks
- They fund both sides of every war
- They are the hidden rulers behind the "New World Order"

**Origin:**
- 1846: French political pamphlet by Georges Dairnvaell (writing as "Satan") about the Rothschild family
- The Waterloo myth specifically was fabricated in this pamphlet and has been proven false
- 1940: Nazi propaganda film "Die Rothschilds" cemented these myths in popular culture
- Builds on medieval antisemitic stereotypes about Jewish moneylending (Jews were often FORCED into finance because they were banned from other professions)

**Debunking:**
- By 2019, only ONE Rothschild (Benjamin de Rothschild) appeared on the Forbes billionaires list -- ranked #1,349. If they "controlled the world's wealth," they would be #1
- The Federal Reserve is a public institution governed by a board appointed by the US President and confirmed by the Senate. It is not "owned" by any family
- The Waterloo story was proven false by historical records showing Nathan Rothschild's actual trades
- The family's wealth, while significant in the 19th century, has been divided among hundreds of descendants and is dwarfed by modern tech billionaires
- PolitiFact rated claims of Rothschild global financial control as FALSE

**Grain of truth being distorted:** The Rothschilds WERE a powerful banking family in the 19th century. They did have branches across Europe. But "a wealthy family" is not "a family that secretly controls the world." Many non-Jewish families (Rockefellers, Medicis, Fuggers) had similar or greater financial power without generating conspiracy theories.

**How widespread:** Extremely. "Rothschild" has become a codeword for antisemitic "Jewish banker" tropes. Used across the political spectrum -- far-right, far-left, Islamist movements, and mainstream figures like Marjorie Taylor Greene.

## A3. The "Dual Loyalty" Conspiracy

**What is claimed:**
Jews are inherently disloyal to the countries they live in. Their true allegiance is to fellow Jews and/or to Israel. Therefore, Jews cannot be trusted as citizens, soldiers, officials, or neighbors. Jewish politicians and officials are secretly working for Israel's interests, not their own country's.

- New Testament: Judas's betrayal of Jesus created the archetype of the "treacherous Jew"

- Stalin's "Doctors' Plot" (1952-53): Jewish doctors accused of conspiring to assassinate Soviet leaders
- Today: Applied to Jewish Americans supporting Israel

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Many Jews DO feel a connection to Israel. But feeling connection to an ancestral homeland is universal among diaspora communities and is fundamentally different from "disloyalty."

**How widespread:** A 2014 ADL global survey found 41% of respondents believed Jews are "more loyal to Israel than to this country" -- making it the single most widely believed antisemitic stereotype worldwide.

## B1. "Israel Did 9/11"

**What is claimed:**
Israel and/or the Mossad orchestrated the September 11, 2001 attacks to draw the United States into wars against Israel's enemies in the Middle East. Key sub-claims:
- Five "Dancing Israelis" were filming and celebrating the attacks, proving foreknowledge
- 4,000 Jews were warned not to go to work at the World Trade Center that day
- Mossad agents were shadowing the hijackers
- Israel benefited from 9/11 (the "cui bono" argument)

**Origin:**
- The "4,000 Jews" claim appeared on the Hezbollah-affiliated Al-Manar television within days of 9/11
- The "Dancing Israelis" story emerged from a police report about 5 Israeli men observed filming from a New Jersey rooftop
- Promoted by conspiracy theorists like Christopher Bollyn, Kevin Barrett, and various white supremacist websites

**Debunking:**
- The "Dancing Israelis" (5 employees of Urban Moving Systems) were detained for 2+ months, subjected to interrogation and polygraph tests, and ultimately found to have NO connection to the attacks. They were deported for immigration violations only
- The "4,000 Jews" claim is completely fabricated. Approximately 400-500 Jewish people died in the attacks. The actual number of Israeli nationals killed was 5
- The 9/11 Commission, FBI, CIA, and multiple independent investigations found ZERO evidence of Israeli involvement
- Al-Qaeda itself claimed responsibility and explained their motives in detail
- Intercepted Israeli military communications from the day show their biggest fear was that they had accidentally attacked a SOVIET ship (USS Liberty context), not American

**Grain of truth being distorted:** The 5 Israeli men WERE briefly detained -- but being detained and being guilty are completely different things. Israel DID benefit from US Middle East policy after 9/11, but benefiting from an event is not evidence of causing it.

**How widespread:** ADL reports that 20 years later, 9/11-Israel conspiracy theories remain a primary recruitment tool for white supremacists. The theories are mainstream in many Arab countries.

## B2. "Israel Created Hamas"

**What is claimed:**
Israel deliberately created Hamas as a tool to divide Palestinians, weaken the PLO, and prevent a Palestinian state. Therefore, Israel is responsible for Hamas terrorism, including October 7.

**Origin:**
- Based on real (but distorted) historical facts about Israel's relationship with Islamic groups in Gaza in the 1970s-80s
- Amplified after October 7, 2023 as a way to blame Israel for its own suffering

**ACTUAL FACTS (this one has genuine complexity):**
- **TRUE**: In the 1970s-80s, Israel allowed and even financially supported Islamic charitable organizations in Gaza run by Sheikh Ahmed Yassin's Mujama al-Islamiya (predecessor of Hamas). Israeli military governor Yitzhak Segev confirmed giving funds to mosques

- **TRUE**: Avner Cohen, former Israeli religious affairs official in Gaza, said: "Hamas, to my great regret, is Israel's creation"
- **TRUE**: Former PM Ehud Barak said Netanyahu's strategy was to keep Hamas "alive and kicking" to weaken the Palestinian Authority

Hamas was founded in 1987 during the First Intifada as an INDEPENDENT outgrowth of the Muslim Brotherhood. Israel did not write its charter, train its fighters, or direct its strategy
- **BUT**: The Muslim Brotherhood existed in Gaza since the 1940s, long before Israeli involvement
- **BUT**: Hamas developed its own ideology, leadership, and military capability independently of Israel
- Israel was supporting CHARITABLE organizations (schools, clinics, mosques) -- which are fundamentally different from military wings

**Grain of truth being distorted:** SIGNIFICANT. Israel DID make a catastrophic strategic error by supporting Islamist groups to weaken the PLO. But "Israel made a strategic mistake" is very different from "Israel created a terrorist organization." The conspiracy version removes all agency from Palestinians and implies Hamas is an Israeli puppet -- which is obviously false given that Hamas's explicit goal is Israel's destruction.

**Grain of truth being distorted:** AIPAC IS influential. Pro-Israel lobbying IS effective. But effective lobbying in a democracy is not "control" -- it's how democracy works. Every interest group lobbies. The conspiracy version implies something sinister about Jews specifically exercising their democratic rights.

## B5. "Israel Poisoned Yasser Arafat"

**What is claimed:**
Israel assassinated Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat by poisoning him with polonium-210 (radioactive material) in 2004.

**Origin:**
- Arafat died November 11, 2004, in a French military hospital at age 75
- His cause of death was never conclusively determined
- In 2012, Al Jazeera reported Swiss scientists found elevated polonium levels on his belongings
- The Palestinian Authority accused Israel of assassination

**Debunking:**
- THREE independent investigation teams examined the evidence (French, Swiss, Russian):
  - French team: concluded there was NOT enough evidence to support poisoning
  - Russian team: found radioactive material was of "environmental nature," not intentional poisoning
  - Swiss team: said findings "moderately support" poisoning but were inconclusive
- French judges officially ruled: "It has not been demonstrated that Mr. Yasser Arafat was murdered by polonium-210 poisoning"
- In 2015, France closed the investigation citing insufficient evidence
- Dr. Ely Karmon noted that polonium-210's half-life (138 days) means that if used in 2004, only traces would remain by 2012 -- yet high levels were found, suggesting contamination occurred AFTER death, not before
- Arafat was 75 years old and had various health conditions

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Arafat's cause of death IS genuinely unclear, and one of three investigation teams found partial evidence. But "unclear cause of death" does not equal "Israel poisoned him," and the majority of investigators concluded poisoning was not proven.

## C1. Classic Blood Libel (Medieval Origin, Still Active)

**What is claimed:**
Jews murder Christian (or Muslim) children and use their blood to bake matzah (unleavened bread) for Passover, or for other religious rituals.

**Origin:**
- 1144 CE: The first recorded blood libel case in Norwich, England (death of William of Norwich)
- Spread throughout medieval Europe
- Led to mass murders of Jews, expulsions, and pogroms for 900 years
- Still published in some Arab media today

**Debunking:**
- Jewish law (Halakha) EXPLICITLY AND ABSOLUTELY prohibits the consumption of blood. Kosher slaughter specifically drains all blood from meat. Blood consumption is one of the strongest prohibitions in Judaism
- No evidence of such rituals has EVER been found in 900 years of accusations
- Multiple Popes investigated and rejected blood libel claims (Innocent IV, 1247; Gregory X, 1272)
- Courts that conducted fair investigations consistently acquitted accused Jews
- The accusation itself violates basic logic: why would a religion that forbids eating animal blood require human blood?

**Grain of truth being distorted:** NONE. Completely fabricated from nothing.

## C2. Modern Blood Libel: "Israel Kills Babies / Targets Children"

**What is claimed:**
Israel deliberately and systematically targets Palestinian children for killing. Israeli soldiers enjoy killing children. Palestinian children are a "favorite target." This is framed as a continuation of historical Jewish bloodlust.

**Origin:**
- Evolved from classic blood libel, adapted for the Israeli-Palestinian conflict
- Amplified massively after October 7, 2023 and the Gaza military operation
- Cartoons in Arab media depicting Israeli leaders drinking Palestinian blood or eating Palestinian children directly echo medieval blood libel imagery

**Debunking:**
- Civilian casualties, including children, occur in ALL armed conflicts. This is tragic but not evidence of "targeting"
- Israel's military uses roof-knocking, phone warnings, leaflet drops, and evacuation corridors -- measures specifically designed to reduce civilian casualties
- The ratio of civilian to combatant casualties in Gaza is comparable to or better than other urban warfare situations (US in Mosul, Coalition in Raqqa)
- Hamas specifically embeds military infrastructure in schools, hospitals, and residential areas, deliberately increasing civilian casualties
- The language of "killing babies" specifically echoes blood libel tropes, suggesting the characterization is influenced by antisemitism rather than genuine concern for civilian protection

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Children DO die in the conflict. This is genuinely horrific and deserves serious humanitarian attention. But "children die in war" is fundamentally different from "Jews target children for killing," and the blood libel framing prevents honest discussion of how to actually protect civilians.

## C3. Organ Harvesting Conspiracy

**What is claimed:**
Israel systematically harvests organs from dead Palestinians (and sometimes other victims). The IDF kills Palestinians specifically to steal their organs. Israel runs an international organ trafficking ring.

**Origin:**
- **1990s scandal (REAL)**: Dr. Yehuda Hiss at the Abu Kabir Forensic Institute harvested organs and tissues from bodies (including IDF soldiers, Palestinians, and foreign workers) WITHOUT family consent. This was a real institutional failure
- **2009**: Swedish tabloid Aftonbladet published Donald Bostrom's article implying IDF kills Palestinians for organs
- **2010**: Israel and IDF confirmed Abu Kabir had stopped the unauthorized practice
- Post-October 7: Allegations resurged claiming organs were harvested from Gazans

**Debunking:**
- The Abu Kabir scandal was REAL but was about unauthorized tissue/cornea collection from ALL bodies (not just Palestinians) by one pathologist. It was not a government program, not targeted at Palestinians, and was stopped when discovered
- Bostrom himself admitted he could not prove his allegations; he "implied" rather than directly accused
- No credible evidence of systematic organ harvesting from Palestinians has ever been produced
- Israel has one of the world's most regulated organ transplant systems since the 2008 Organ Transplant Law
- The conspiracy specifically echoes medieval blood libel (Jews taking body parts from non-Jews)

**Grain of truth being distorted:** SIGNIFICANT. The Abu Kabir scandal was real and disturbing. But "one pathologist violated protocol with bodies of all backgrounds" was transformed into "Israel systematically murders Palestinians to harvest their organs" -- a massive distortion.

## E1. October 7 False Flag / Denial

- International journalists attended screenings of Hamas body-cam footage
- Forensic evidence was examined by international teams

**Grain of truth being distorted:** There ARE legitimate questions about Israeli intelligence failures before October 7 (how did they not see it coming?). And there ARE documented cases of Israeli tank fire hitting buildings where hostages were held. But intelligence failure and fog-of-war tragedies are fundamentally different from "Israel orchestrated the attack."

## E2. General "Mossad Did It" False Flag Culture

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Israel's Mossad IS a highly capable intelligence agency that has conducted real covert operations (including assassinations). The Lavon Affair WAS a real false flag attempt. But capability does not equal responsibility for every event.

## S1. "Holocaust Numbers Were Exaggerated"

**What is claimed:**
Far fewer than six million Jews died in the Holocaust. Common claims include: only 271,000 died (based on a misrepresented Red Cross document), the number was inflated for political purposes, or the true figure is "only" in the hundreds of thousands. This is often presented as "just asking questions" rather than outright denial.

**Origin:**
- Holocaust denial emerged almost immediately after WWII, promoted by former Nazis and their sympathizers
- Paul Rassinier (French, 1960s), Robert Faurisson (French), David Irving (British), and the Institute for Historical Review (US) are key promoters
- The "Red Cross document" claim misuses a 1979 document from the Special Registry Office in Bad Arolsen, Germany
- Growing on social media through "soft denial" that questions specific numbers without denying the Holocaust outright

**How widespread:**
- Survey data shows alarming levels of Holocaust denial or minimization:
  - In the US, 23% of respondents aged 18-39 believe the Holocaust is a myth or greatly exaggerated (Claims Conference survey, 2020)
- Illegal in many European countries (Germany, France, Austria, Belgium, etc.)
- Still promoted openly in some countries (Iran hosted a Holocaust denial conference in 2006)
- Growing on social media platforms despite content moderation

**Debunking:**
- **The Red Cross document**: The 1979 document explicitly states: "The notarization figures of the Special Registry Office do not allow any conclusions to be drawn on the actual number of deaths in the concentration camps." It only reflects death CERTIFICATES issued -- not total deaths. Many victims were never registered, shot in mass executions, or killed in death camps that destroyed records
- The ICRC (International Committee of the Red Cross) stated: "The ICRC accepts the Holocaust as fact and does not dispute in any way the official number of lives that were lost"
- The six million figure is based on:
  - Nazi documentation of deportation transports and camp records
  - Census comparisons (pre-war vs. post-war Jewish populations)
  - German wartime reports (Einsatzgruppen reports meticulously documented their killings)
  - Post-war investigations and trials (Nuremberg, Eichmann trial)
  - Testimonies from thousands of survivors AND perpetrators
  - Archaeological evidence (mass graves, cremation sites)
  - The Arolsen Archives (International Center on Nazi Persecution) contain 30 million documents
- The vast majority of scholarly studies provide an estimate between 5 and 7 million Jewish victims
- SS officer Wilhelm Hoettl testified at Nuremberg that Adolf Eichmann personally told him 6 million Jews had been killed

**Grain of truth being distorted:** The exact number of Holocaust victims IS debated by historians -- estimates range from 5.1 to 6.9 million. But debating whether the number is 5.7 or 6.2 million is legitimate scholarship. Claiming "only" 271,000 died is denial, not scholarship.

**Sources:** [Full Fact: Red Cross Documents](https://fullfact.org/online/holocaust-death-toll/) | [USHMM: Evidence and Documentation](https://www.ushmm.org/antisemitism/holocaust-denial-and-distortion/evidence-documentation-holocaust) | [ADL: Debunking Holocaust Denial Claims](https://www.adl.org/resources/backgrounder/debunking-holocaust-denial-claims)

## S2. "Gas Chambers Were Delousing Chambers"

**What is claimed:**
The gas chambers at Auschwitz and other camps were actually delousing facilities used to combat typhus by disinfecting clothing with Zyklon B. They were never used to kill people. The "Leuchter Report" supposedly proved this by finding low cyanide residue levels in the gas chambers.

**Origin:**
- Fred Leuchter produced his "report" in 1988 for the defense in the Ernst Zundel Holocaust denial trial in Canada
- Robert Faurisson and other deniers had previously made similar claims
- The argument rests on comparing cyanide residue levels in the gas chambers vs. the delousing chambers

**How widespread:**
- Central claim of "technical" Holocaust denial
- Promoted by the Institute for Historical Review and David Irving (before his conviction)
- Common on Holocaust denial websites and forums
- Used as a "scientific" veneer for denial

**Debunking:**
- **Leuchter's credentials**: When questioned, he was found NOT to be a licensed engineer. His degree was in the humanities. He had NO expertise in chemistry, toxicology, or cremation. The court refused to accept his report as evidence
- **The chemistry explained**: Insects have FAR higher resistance to cyanide than humans. Delousing requires concentrations up to **16,000 ppm** and long exposure times. KILLING HUMANS requires only **300 ppm** for a few minutes. This fully explains why delousing chambers have more cyanide residue -- the exposure was orders of magnitude higher
- **Physical evidence**: Surviving SS construction documents from 1943 refer to "gas-tight doors" for the Birkenau crematoria, with dimensions matching the gas chambers
- **Perpetrator testimony**: Rudolf Hoss (Auschwitz commandant) described the gassing process in detail. Numerous SS members testified about the gas chambers
- **Eyewitness accounts**: Thousands of survivors and camp workers described the gas chambers
- **Sonderkommando testimony**: Jewish prisoners forced to operate the crematoria provided detailed descriptions of the gassing process, including the discovery of the Sonderkommando scrolls buried at Auschwitz
- A 1994 report by the Institute of Forensic Research in Krakow found cyanide compounds in gas chamber walls, confirming their use for killing

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Zyklon B WAS used for delousing -- that was its original commercial purpose. The Nazis then repurposed it as a mass murder weapon. The existence of delousing chambers does not disprove the existence of gas chambers; both existed for different purposes.

**Sources:** [Auschwitz Museum: Leuchter Report](https://www.auschwitz.org/en/history/holocaust-denial/leuchter-report/) | [USHMM: Gassing Operations](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/gassing-operations) | [Holocaust Denial on Trial: Leuchter Report](https://www.hdot.org/debunking-denial/ab12-leuchter-report/)

## S3. "Jews Declared War on Germany First"

**What is claimed:**
Jews "declared war" on Germany in 1933 -- before any persecution occurred -- proving that Jews were the aggressors and the Nazis acted in self-defense. The evidence cited is a March 24, 1933 Daily Express (London) headline: "JUDEA DECLARES WAR ON GERMANY."

**Origin:**
- The Daily Express published a sensationalist front page on March 24, 1933 about a Jewish-organized economic boycott of German goods
- Nazi propaganda minister Joseph Goebbels used the boycott as justification for the Nazi boycott of Jewish businesses on April 1, 1933
- Holocaust deniers and revisionists have used this headline for decades to portray Jews as aggressors

**How widespread:**
- The Daily Express front page is one of the most widely shared images in Holocaust denial circles
- Common on conspiracy websites, social media, and forums
- Used to create the narrative that Jews "started it" and the Holocaust was retaliation

**Debunking:**
- **Timeline is critical**: Hitler became Chancellor on January 30, 1933. By March 24:
  - The Reichstag Fire had already occurred (Feb 27)
  - The Enabling Act gave Hitler dictatorial powers (March 23 -- ONE DAY before the headline)
  - SA brownshirts were already attacking Jews, boycotting Jewish stores, and destroying property
  - Jews were being purged from civil service, universities, and professions
- **The "declaration of war" was an economic boycott** -- a nonviolent protest. The article text itself says so. "War" was sensationalist newspaper language for a boycott
- **"Judea" is not a country** and cannot "declare war." There was no Jewish state, no Jewish army, no Jewish government. The boycott was organized by diaspora organizations with no military power whatsoever
- **The boycott was a RESPONSE to Nazi persecution**, not the cause of it. The sequence is clear: Nazi persecution came first --> Jewish boycott was a response
- **Blaming the victim**: This argument is like saying a mugging victim "started the fight" by calling the police
- The Nazi boycott of Jewish businesses (April 1, 1933) was planned BEFORE the Jewish boycott was announced

**Grain of truth being distorted:** The Daily Express headline is real, and Jewish organizations did organize an economic boycott of Germany in 1933. But it was a DEFENSIVE, NONVIOLENT response to the Nazis' violent persecution of Jews, not an act of aggression.

## S4. "Hitler Was Actually Jewish"

**What is claimed:**
Adolf Hitler had Jewish ancestry, making the Holocaust a "Jewish civil war" or proving that "Jews are their own worst enemy." The main version claims Hitler's paternal grandmother (Maria Schicklgruber) was impregnated by a Jewish man named Frankenberger in Graz, Austria.

**Origin:**
- The "Frankenberger thesis" was created by Hans Frank, Hitler's personal lawyer, in his 1946 memoir *Im Angesicht des Galgens* (In the Face of the Gallows), written while awaiting execution at Nuremberg

- A 2010 study by journalist Jean-Paul Mulders claimed Hitler's DNA showed Berber and Jewish markers (widely disputed)

**How widespread:**
- One of the most persistent myths about Hitler
- Used by antisemites to blame Jews for the Holocaust ("a Jew killed other Jews")
- Used by some to trivialize the Holocaust
- Appears frequently in popular culture and social media

**Debunking:**
- **No Jews lived in Graz in 1837**: Historian Brigitte Hamann documented that Jews had been expelled from Styria (including Graz) in the 15th century and were not permitted to return until the 1860s -- decades AFTER Maria Schicklgruber became pregnant
- **No Frankenberger family found**: Extensive searches have failed to locate any Jewish family named Frankenberger in Graz at the relevant time
- **No correspondence found**: Frank claimed letters existed between the families; no such correspondence has ever been found
- **Frank's credibility**: He was writing while under sentence of death, with obvious motivation to sensationalize and deflect blame
- **2025 DNA analysis**: A new documentary presented DNA analysis of Hitler's blood that "firmly refuted the Frankenberger thesis," showing Austrian-German ancestry with no trace of Jewish lineage
- Prominent historians (Ian Kershaw, Brigitte Hamann, Richard Evans) have rejected the Frankenberger thesis

**Grain of truth being distorted:** Hitler's paternal grandfather's identity IS genuinely unknown -- Maria Schicklgruber was unmarried when she gave birth to Alois Hitler. This mystery has fueled speculation. But uncertainty about a grandfather's identity ≠ evidence of Jewish ancestry.
